❓ Credit Card Rewards FAQ

What is the 4 MPD rule in Singapore?

The 4 MPD (miles per dollar) rule refers to using specialized credit cards that offer 4 miles for every S$1 spent on specific categories like online shopping or dining, rather than a base rate of 1.2-1.4 MPD.

Are air miles better than cashback?

Miles are generally better for high-value travel (Business/First class), while cashback is better for immediate savings and low-to-mid level spenders who don't travel often.

How do I avoid credit card annual fees?

Most banks in Singapore allow you to waive annual fees if you meet a minimum spend or call their customer service hotline. Some cards like HSBC Revolution have no annual fee at all.
